Online shade photos don't show how a lipstick looks on you

  • A small photo is a poor guide. Colour changes with lighting, screens and your own skin undertone.
  • Wrong shades go unused. People buy several colours online, and the ones that don't suit them never get worn.
  • Custom lipstick is expensive. The YSL custom lipstick device sells at $350 in the United States, and tools like it are hard to find in Sri Lanka.

Privacy, and what to expect

Your photo stays with you

The scan is read in your browser and the picture is discarded. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Secure accounts

Passwords are hashed and the site uses HTTPS. We design around UK GDPR and Sri Lanka's PDPA No. 9 of 2022.

Honest limits

The machine is simulated while hardware is prepared. The prototype uses food-safe test colour, not cosmetic-grade pigment.

Questions

Is it safe to wear?

Not yet. The prototype dispenses food-safe test colour only. A real product would need cosmetic-grade pigments and safety testing.

What happens to my photo?

It is analysed in your browser to read skin and outfit colour, then discarded. We never save it.

Do I need the machine?

No. You can scan, design and save shades on the website. The machine page is a simulation until the hardware is connected.

How are shades suggested?

From your skin depth, undertone and outfit colour, using a rule table. It is a suggestion, and you can adjust every slider.
