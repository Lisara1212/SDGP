Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. A short, plain-language description of what AuraBlend stores and why.

Who we are

AuraBlend (aurablend.lk) is a university project by SDGP Group 68. It is a prototype lipstick studio: you scan a photo, get a suggested pigment mix, save shades, and request a mixing machine. It is not a commercial service yet.

Contact: aurablendlk@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign up with an email and password, the password is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash, never as plain text. If you use Continue with Google, we receive only your name and email address from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Your content: shades you save, machine jobs you send, cartridge levels in the demo machine, and whether you asked for the machine.
  • Preferences: your light or dark mode choice.

The early list (waitlist)

  • What: the email address (and optional first name) you type into the early-list form on the home page, the date you joined, and your agreement to be contacted.
  • Why: only to tell you when the AuraBlend machine is ready and to gauge interest in the project. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.
  • Where: in a secured Google Cloud Firestore database that only the AuraBlend team can read.
  • How long: until the project ends or you ask us to remove you, and in any case no later than 12 months after the project's final assessment. Then it is deleted.
  • Withdraw: email us at the address above and we will delete your entry within 14 days.

What we do not collect

  • Photos. The camera and uploaded pictures are read inside your own browser to estimate a skin and outfit colour, and are then discarded. They are never uploaded or stored.
  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, and no selling of data.

Where your data is kept

In this demo stage, account data and saved shades are kept in your own browser (local storage) on the device you use. Clearing your browser data removes them. When the AuraBlend back end goes live, this information will be kept in a secured database, and we will update this page before that happens.

Other services we use

  • Google: for Continue with Google, and to load the website fonts. Google may receive your IP address when these load. See Google's own privacy policy.
  • Vercel: hosts the website and may keep normal server logs such as IP address and time of visit.

How long we keep your information

  • Accounts and saved shades: kept until you delete them (Profile, then Delete my account and data), or until you clear your browser data in the demo stage.
  • Early-list emails: see above.
  • Photos: not kept at all.

Your choices

  • Download or delete: log in, open Profile, and use Download my data or Delete my account and data. Deleting removes your account, shades and jobs.
  • Google access: you can remove AuraBlend at any time in your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services.
  • Access, correction or deletion: email us at the address above and tell us what you want. We reply within 14 days.

Safety note

The prototype machine mixes food-safe test colour to show the idea. It is not real lipstick and is not for use on lips.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and its date. This page describes a student prototype and is provided for information.
