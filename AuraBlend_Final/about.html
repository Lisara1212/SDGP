ABOUT US

Building the Future of Personalized Beauty

AuraBlend combines beauty and technology through an intelligent lipstick customization platform. Our mission is to empower users to create unique shades, save their formulas, and produce them seamlessly using IoT-powered lipstick blending technology.

Our Journey

AuraBlend started as a Software Development Group Project with the vision of transforming traditional cosmetic customization. We recognized the challenge users face when searching for their perfect lipstick shade and developed a solution that combines customization, formula storage, and automated production into one connected platform.

Our Mission

To redefine cosmetic personalization by making lipstick creation more accessible, accurate, and innovative through smart technology and IoT integration.
